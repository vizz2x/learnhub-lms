## ENHANCED PRODUCTION NOTES:

### Child Voice Characteristics:
- **SARA (10, Girl):** Thoughtful, slightly quieter, observant
- **MARCUS (9, Boy):** High energy, enthusiastic, competitive
- **LILY (12, Girl):** Mature for age, articulate, confident
- **JAMES (8, Boy):** Honest, playful, younger sounding

### Recording Strategy:
1. Record adult narration first in one complete take for consistency
2. Record child voices separately with multiple takes per line
3. Choose most natural-sounding child takes (avoid "acting")
4. Mix child voices at 85-90% volume of adult for natural interjection feel
5. Add slight reverb to adult voice for authority, keep child voices dry for authenticity

### Music Cues:
- **Intro (Slides 1-2):** Inspirational, building
- **Strategy (Slides 3-8):** Steady, professional, confident
- **Enhancements (Slides 9-15):** Uplifting, slightly more energetic
- **Technical (Slides 16-19):** Minimal music, let content speak
- **Closing (Slide 20):** Return to inspirational theme, crescendo at end

### Emotional Arc:
Create journey from "professional pitch" → "human impact" → "technical credibility" → "inspiring call-to-action"

Child voices should feel spontaneous, not scripted - like they're interrupting naturally with genuine reactions.

## PRODUCTION NOTES:

### Voice Casting:
- **Adult Voice:** Male or female, age 35-50, warm but authoritative tone. Think documentary narrator or TED talk speaker. Should convey expertise and passion without sounding condescending.

- **Child Voice:** Age 8-12, naturally enthusiastic without being overly cutesy. Should sound genuine, not acted. Use different children for variety if possible (3-4 different voices throughout).

### Timing Notes:
- Total presentation runtime: Approximately 12-15 minutes
- Each slide: 45-90 seconds average
- Child interjections: 2-4 seconds each
- Maintain steady pacing, don't rush

### Technical Direction:
- Adult voice should be recorded in a professional studio for clarity
- Child voices can have slightly more ambient sound to feel authentic
- Use subtle background music (inspirational/educational) at 20% volume
- Increase music during transitions between major sections
- Fade out music completely during child interjections for emphasis

### Emotional Beats:
- Start strong and confident (Slide 1-2)
- Build excitement through phases (Slide 3-8)
- Peak enthusiasm at enhancements (Slide 9-15)
- Professional authority on technical details (Slide 16-19)
- Inspiring and hopeful conclusion (Slide 20)

### Child Voice Placement Strategy:
- Use child voices to validate key benefits
- Place after adult makes technical claim to humanize it
- Keep to 1-2 interjections per slide maximum
- Save most enthusiastic child reactions for gamification and AI tutor slides
- Use different child voices to suggest diverse user base
